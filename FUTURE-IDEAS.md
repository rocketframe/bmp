# Future ideas

## A cup for shared leagues
T's idea (2026-11-11): a knockout cup for the top 64 clubs of the pyramid, played between league matchdays.
- With four divisions of 16 to 20 clubs (64 to 80 clubs), the cup takes the top 64 by division and last
  season's position: 6 rounds (64, 32, 16, 8, semi-finals, final).
- Cup rounds would sit in the schedule as extra matchdays (for example every fifth day), played live like league
  matchdays, with extra time and penalties.
- Prize money and gate receipts per round; lower-division clubs get a big payday from a home draw against a
  top club.
- Needs: a cup draw and bracket in the state, fixtures that are not tied to one division, penalties in the
  match sim, and a cup screen.
